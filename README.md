## About me

I usually go by **WhyHorsey** online, but my real name is **Galang**, a student exploring the world of **cybersecurity, Linux, and free and open-source software**.

I'm still *really* early in the journey, so this profile isn't a showcase of me knowing everything. It's more like a public notebook of the things I'm learning, building, configuring, and occasionally troubleshooting at 2 AM (lol).

I like understanding **why something works**, not just copying a command and hoping for the best. That means documenting the process, keeping track of mistakes, and writing notes that make sense to future me (and hopefully to someone else, too).

## What I'm working on

- 📚 **[linux-notes](https://github.com/WhyHorsey/linux-notes)** — Personal notes on Linux, Git, SSH, and networking. I try to explain things in a way that's makes sense to me, but I also try not to make it too complicated either, this means that the notes will sometimes be filled with analogies and practical lessons from my own learning process.
- ⚙️ **[dotfiles](https://github.com/WhyHorsey/dotfiles)** — My Linux configuration, a *"rice"* setup, if you wanna call it that maybe, including shell and terminal tools, desktop tweaks, and other settings. I use GNU Stow to manage the configurations.
- 🖥️ **[acer-homelab](https://github.com/WhyHorsey/acer-homelab)** — A work-in-progress log of my small home server: self-hosted services, Docker Compose, remote access, backups, and the lessons that come with keeping everything running.
- 🎮 **[limbus-bot](https://github.com/WhyHorsey/limbus-bot)** — Another WIP project. It autoamates the process of doing farming stages in a game called Limbus Company.

## How I like to learn

I learn best by trying things hands on, figuring out what went wrong, and writing down what I discover. My notes aim to answer more than just **“what command should I run?”** They should also explain **what it does, why it works, what it changes, and what to watch out for**. This is especially useful when I sometimes get overwhelmed by the amount of information that I'm getting.
